## How to score

Grounded = Yes when every factual claim is supported by the retrieved text, or the model correctly refuses an unsupported question. A fabricated fact, unsupported personal eligibility claim, or refusal when an answer is present = No. Minor missing conditions are described in Notes.

Retrieval quality: 5 = all necessary evidence present; 4 = main evidence present with minor omissions; 3 = partial evidence; 2 = mostly irrelevant; 1 = no relevant passage. For unsupported questions, a 5 requires an explicit absence statement; a 1 means no evidence exists, which is expected and does not mean refusal failed.

## Iterations and practical limitations

The first 1.5-billion-parameter model with a lengthy JSON-oriented prompt refused all 15 questions despite relevant retrieval. A shorter prompt with the question after the passages improved answerability, but several exact citations were invalid. Switching to qwen2.5:3b yielded valid exact source quotations for all 10 supported answers in the final run. Missing inline citation labels are filled from verified source IDs; invalid IDs or quotes are still rejected.

Questions 11 and 12 remain generation failures. Rephrasing into narrower questions is a practical demo workaround; a larger model or optional OpenAI backend could be evaluated later. They were not silently removed or counted as successes. Question 10 demonstrates why a valid quotation cannot prove personal eligibility.

Retrieved windows overlap, so top results can repeat material. For meals, chunk 2 contains the end of the meal section while chunk 1 contains the actual dollar limit; the top-4 context correctly includes both. Repeated home-office rules mean an answer may need only the expense document even though both policy documents are retrieved.

Browser verification confirmed the PTO answer and expense answer with filename/chunk/quote, and the unsupported CEO refusal. A disposable two-page PDF verified page metadata, actual text extraction and semantic retrieval. Additional checks passed for invalid TXT warnings, chunk bounds, normalized 384-dimensional vectors, index reuse and restoration of the three-document sample corpus.
